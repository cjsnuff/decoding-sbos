---
name: Complementary promotions
description: User's scope and presentation constraints for promoting Fluency alongside Decoding the sbOS.
---

Fluency is an optional, complementary experience, not a prerequisite for Decoding the sbOS. Its promotion must not replace or displace the main sbOS offer. Enrollment-credit conditions must be visible and readable, never hidden in a tooltip.

**Why:** The user explicitly set these boundaries when requesting the Fluency promotion.

**How to apply:** Preserve this product hierarchy when revising either offer or adding future promotions. Keep participation requirements next to the credit claim.
